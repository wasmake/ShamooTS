# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful technical disagreement, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what benefits the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information, or conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub profiles or repository security contact. Maintainers will investigate promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may correct, warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants according to severity and pattern. Enforcement decisions may be appealed to a maintainer not involved in the original decision when one is available.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
